[Check before downloading the text-to-3D models]

The feature that generates 3D from text uses OpenAI's CLIP
(openai/clip-vit-large-patch14) to analyze the text you enter.
Before downloading it, please check the following points.

1. The model's distribution page on Hugging Face does not state a license.
   (The openai/CLIP repository on GitHub, which publishes the code, is under the
   MIT License.)
2. The model card states: "The model is intended as a research output for research
   communities."
   It states: "Any deployed use case of the model - whether commercial or not - is
   currently out of scope."
   It explicitly states: "Certain use cases which would fall under the domain of
   surveillance and facial recognition are always out-of-scope regardless of
   performance of the model."
   It states: "Since the model has not been purposefully trained in or evaluated on any
   languages other than English, its use should be limited to English language use
   cases."
   (Please enter your text in English in this tool as well.)
3. This tool uses CLIP only to convert the text you enter into the condition for the
   3D generation model.
   It does not use CLIP to classify images or to identify people.
4. What will be downloaded:
   - openai/clip-vit-large-patch14 (commit 32bd6428): configuration, weights and
     tokenizer, 7 files, about 1.71 GB
   - microsoft/TRELLIS-text-xlarge (MIT License): weights, about 4.13 GB
   They are saved inside the setup destination folder (in models and hf-home).

Model card: https://huggingface.co/openai/clip-vit-large-patch14

Even if you do not agree, generating 3D from images remains available as it is.
